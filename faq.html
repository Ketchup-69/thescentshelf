Quick answers

Before you reserve.

What is The Scent Shelf?
An online perfume shop for India, opening soon. Most of the shelf is affordable perfume from Arabic houses such as Lattafa, Afnan, Rasasi and Armaf, including perfumes that smell like well-known designer bottles. Designer and niche perfumes are on a separate shelf called Lux.
Is anything on sale yet?
Not yet. Every perfume is marked 'Coming soon'. You can join the waitlist or reserve bottles now, and nothing is charged until we launch and confirm your order.
What does "smells like" mean?
It names the designer perfume a bottle is most often compared to. The two are different perfumes made by different houses. We are not connected to, or endorsed by, the designer brands.
Are the perfumes genuine?
We only list genuine bottles from the houses named on them. If a bottle you receive is damaged, wrong or not genuine, you can claim a replacement or refund under our refund policy.
How does buy 5, get 1 free work?
Put six bottles in your bag and the cheapest one is free. Delivery on that order is free too. Every sixth bottle in an order is free.
How much is delivery?
A flat delivery fee is shown in your bag before you reserve. It is free on any order that includes a free bottle.
Can I return a perfume?
All sales are final once paid, except for damaged, wrong or non-genuine items, which you can claim within the window set out in our refund policy.
How long do these perfumes last?
Each perfume page shows our longevity estimate out of 5. Many Arabic perfumes are known for lasting a long time, but heat, skin and how much you spray all make a difference.
Why are prices in rupees and dollars?
You pay in rupees. Dollars are shown for reference, at about ₹88 to the dollar.
What happens to my details?
We use them only for your reservation and launch updates. We never sell them, and we delete them when you ask. Details are in our privacy notice.
What is founder hand-delivery?
An optional extra at checkout: the founder brings your order to your door personally. It costs $4,000 and is never selected for you.
