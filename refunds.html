Refunds and cancellations

All sales are final

Once an order is paid, it is final. We do not accept returns, exchanges or refunds because you changed your mind, did not like the smell, or ordered the wrong one. Perfume is a personal product and cannot be resold once it leaves us. Use the full profile and comparison on each perfume page before you order.

When we will make it right

We will replace the item or refund it in full if:

  • it arrives damaged, leaking or broken;
  • you received a different perfume from the one you ordered; or
  • it is not genuine.

To claim, email [TO CONFIRM: contact email] within [TO CONFIRM: 48 hours] of delivery with your order reference, photos, and an unboxing video showing the sealed parcel being opened. Refunds go back to the original payment method within [TO CONFIRM: 5 to 7 working days] of approval. This does not limit any rights you have under Indian consumer law.

Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel a paid order before it is dispatched for a full refund. Once it has been dispatched, it is final.

Reservations (now)

We are pre-launch. Reserving perfumes and joining the waitlist are free and no payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund. To cancel a reservation or leave the waitlist, email [TO CONFIRM: contact email].

Founder hand-delivery

The optional founder hand-delivery add-on is final once the delivery date is agreed, except where we fail to deliver, in which case it is refunded in full.

Priority deposit (not currently offered)
