This notice explains what we collect when you join The Scent Shelf waitlist, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

The Scent Shelf is operated by [TO CONFIRM: registered business name], [TO CONFIRM: registered address in India]. Contact: [TO CONFIRM: contact email].

What we collect

  • Your name.
  • A phone number or an email address (whichever you give us).
  • Your city.
  • The perfumes you are interested in.
  • The date and time you joined, and a record that you ticked the consent box.

That is all. We do not collect payment details on the waitlist, and this site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about your waitlist request and launch updates, which is what you consent to when you tick the box.
  • To understand demand. We may share totals with suppliers (for example, how many people want a given perfume). We never share your name or contact details with them.

Where it is kept

Waitlist entries are stored in a private Google Sheet in our Google account. Google stores this on our behalf and may process it on servers outside India. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads open-source code libraries from the jsDelivr network; like any web host, both receive your IP address when your browser requests the page.
